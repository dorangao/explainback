## Why both copyright and trademark matter

The U.S. Copyright Office explains that names and short titles are generally outside copyright protection, while original artwork, photographs and sufficiently creative logo artwork can be protected. Names may be protected as trademarks. The USPTO describes trademarks as identifiers of the source of goods or services. Renaming lessons and adding attribution do not establish a license, trademark clearance, or a blanket right to reuse third-party media.
